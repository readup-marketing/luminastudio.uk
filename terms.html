3. Paying

You pay online by card through our secure payment provider, after your taster and before your first paid session. Single sessions are paid in advance. Modules are paid in full in advance unless we agree an instalment plan with you in writing.

4. Rescheduling and missed sessions

  • You can move a session free of charge if you tell us at least 24 hours before it starts.
  • With less than 24 hours' notice, or if your child does not join, the session counts as used. We will always try to help in a genuine emergency.
  • If we have to cancel or move a session, we will offer another time or a full refund for that session.
  • If your child cannot join within 10 minutes of the start, we will treat the session as missed.

5. Your 14-day right to cancel

Because you book online, you have a legal right to cancel within 14 days of booking paid tuition, without giving a reason. To cancel, email hello@luminastudio.uk with your name and the booking.

If you ask us to start sessions within the 14 days, we will ask you to confirm that. If you then cancel within the 14 days, we will refund what you paid, minus the full price of any sessions already delivered and the cost of any learning pack and homework book already posted to you (unless you return them unused). Refunds are paid to the original card within 14 days.

6. Cancelling a module after 14 days

You can stop at any time by emailing us. We will refund any unused sessions from a module, minus the difference between the module price and the single-session price for the sessions already used, and minus the cost of packs already posted. For example, if you have used 4 sessions of a £400 module, we charge those 4 at £40 each (£160) and refund £240, less [£XX] for the posted pack.

  • Sessions use a private link and are not recorded.
  • If we have a concern about a child's safety or wellbeing, we will follow our safeguarding policy, which may mean sharing information with the appropriate authorities.
  • We may end tuition if these rules are not followed, refunding any unused sessions.
