The studio experience

Two labs. One
complete education.

Every week your child attends both a Reading Lab and a Writing Lab — each 45 minutes, each with a distinct purpose that feeds the other.

RL
Twice weekly
Reading Lab

Deep comprehension work anchored to rich, knowledge-building passages. Children don't just read — they analyse, infer and interrogate text. As knowledge grows, so does the quality and depth of everything they write.

45 minutes · Comprehension · Knowledge Building
WL
Twice weekly
Writing Lab

Structured writing sessions that move from sentence to paragraph to argument. Literary devices, register, precision and structure — taught systematically so children have the tools to turn their knowledge into powerful, purposeful writing.

45 minutes · Structure · Literary Craft
Progress & Reporting

Two assessment points.
Full transparency, every time.

At Week 6 and Week 12, every child completes a formal Big Write assessment — an independent piece of extended writing that draws on everything learned in the studio so far. Within a week of each assessment, parents receive a comprehensive written report.

Week 6
Mid-term Assessment

Halfway through the programme, your child completes their first Big Write. The report that follows gives you a clear picture of progress made, skills developing and where to focus for the second half of the term.

  • Independent extended writing piece
  • Teacher assessment against level criteria
  • Written strengths report
  • Identified areas for development
+
Week 12
End-of-Term Assessment

The final Big Write marks the close of the 12-week journey. The end-of-term report gives a complete view of the whole programme — how far your child has come, what they have mastered and what they are ready to tackle next.

  • Independent extended writing piece
  • Full-programme progress review
  • Level recommendation for the next term
  • Personalised next steps
✦ All reports are written by your child's qualified teacher — not generated automatically. You will always know exactly where your child stands.
