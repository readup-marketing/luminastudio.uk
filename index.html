complete education.

Deep comprehension work anchored to rich, knowledge-building passages. Luminaries don't just read — they analyse, infer and interrogate text. As knowledge grows, so does the quality and depth of everything they write.

Progress & Reporting

Two assessment points.
Full transparency, every time.

At Week 6 and Week 12, every Luminary completes a formal Big Write — an independent extended piece that draws on everything learned so far. Within a week, parents receive a comprehensive written report. Posted, not emailed.

Week 6
Mid-term Assessment

Halfway through the programme your child completes their first Big Write. The report that follows gives a clear picture of progress made, skills developing and where to focus for the second half.

  • Independent extended writing piece
  • Teacher assessment against level criteria
  • Written strengths report — posted home
  • Identified areas for development
+
Week 12
End-of-Term Assessment

The final Big Write marks the close of the 12-week journey. The report covers the full programme — how far your child has come, what they have mastered and what they are ready for next term.

  • Independent extended writing piece
  • Full-programme progress review
  • Level recommendation for next term
  • Personalised next steps — posted home
✦ All reports are written by your child's qualified teacher — not generated automatically. Every parent will always know exactly where their child stands.
