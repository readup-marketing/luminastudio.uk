Questions

What parents ask.

Will my 5 or 6-year-old actually focus in an online session?
A full 45 minutes online is a fair question at 5 or 6. It is live one-to-one specialist teaching with a qualified teacher, not a passive video. The free taster is how you see that for yourself.
What if my child doesn't enjoy the taster?
You decide it is not right, and you stop. A short note and a level recommendation still come with that decision. The taster is free either way.
How is this different from a private tutor?
Most tutoring is general homework help. Lumina is specialist English for a child who has struggled with it. Reading and writing are in every session. The aim is stronger reading and writing, and clearer expectations, so you know where to help. A taster pack is posted first. The module adds a learning pack and homework book. A qualified, experienced teacher, not a university student. Five levels, by ability. One or two sessions a week.
Is online tuition as effective as face-to-face?
Sessions are one-to-one and online. Your child stays in a familiar place, and there is no travel. The free taster shows you exactly how that works in practice.
What level will my child be placed at?
It comes with your decision after the taster, in a short note and a level recommendation. Children are placed by ability, not by age, across five levels. You can see what good English looks like for your child.
When can my child start?
You do not wait for a term date. Book the free taster, get the pack, pick a time by email, and attend. If it is right, book one or two sessions a week. Pay £40 a session, or £400 for 12. At one a week the module is about 12 weeks. At two a week, about 6.
What does my child need for the session?
A quiet space, a device with a camera and microphone, and the taster pack we post to you. We'll send a simple link for the session.

Lumina Studios is specialist online one-to-one English tuition for primary school children ages 5–11 across the United Kingdom who have struggled with English. It is hard for parents to know where to help at home. Maths is often easier to support at home. English is harder, and the expectations are harder to see. The aim is stronger reading and writing, and clearer expectations. Every 45-minute session includes reading and writing, with a qualified experienced teacher. Reading Lab and Writing Lab are the two parts of that same session. Five levels, placed by ability. Parents choose one or two sessions a week. The path is simple: book a free online taster on the form, receive a taster pack in the post, check email to pick a time and attend the free taster, decide if it is right, then book sessions. The taster is free either way. A single session is £40. A module of 12 sessions is £400 and saves £80 versus 12 times £40. The module lasts about 12 weeks at one session a week or about 6 weeks at two a week, and includes an English learning pack and a homework book posted after payment.
