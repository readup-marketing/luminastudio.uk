Why Lumina exists

You already know
your child has
more to give.

You see it at home — the curiosity, the questions, the capacity that doesn't always show up in a school report. The gap between where they are and where they could be isn't a talent gap.

It's an expectations gap. Lumina exists to close it. Not by doing more of what school already does — but by holding a higher standard, in a different environment, with a system built to match the child in front of it.

This is not for every child. It is for the child whose parent has always believed there was more in them.

Level, not year group

Children are placed at the level they are actually at — not where their school year puts them. A child in Year 5 reading at Year 3 level gets a Year 3 workbook. No embarrassment. No ceiling. Just the right challenge at the right moment.

Reading Lab + Writing Lab

One session builds knowledge through rich comprehension passages. The other turns that knowledge into structured, precise writing. Two labs, two purposes, one complete education in English — twice a week, 45 minutes each.

Reports posted home

At Week 6 and Week 12, every parent receives a written assessment report from their child's qualified teacher. Not a text at the door. A proper written report — posted to your house — telling you exactly where your child stands and what comes next.

The Lumina Passport

Every child receives a physical Lumina Passport — a permanent record of their journey through all five levels. It is stamped after every assessment, updated at every level. The taster session is where that journey begins.
