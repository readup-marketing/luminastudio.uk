Who we are

Lumina Studios ("we", "us") provides online one-to-one English tuition for children aged 5–11. Lumina Studios is run by Conor Govey, [sole trader / limited company name and number], [business postal address]. We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

Contact us about your data at hello@luminastudio.uk. [ICO registration number: ZXXXXXXX]

What we collect

  • About you (the parent or carer): your name, email address, phone number (if you give it) and postal address.
  • About your child: first name, year group, the areas of English you are worried about, and any learning needs you choose to tell us about so we can teach well.
  • Learning records: assessment results, notes on progress, reports we write for you, and work your child completes in their workbooks.
  • Bookings and payments: the sessions you book and what you have paid. Card payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see or store your full card details.
  • Messages: emails and other messages between you and us.

We only collect information about your child from you, the parent or carer. We do not ask children to give us their personal details.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis (UK GDPR)

  • To provide tuition you have asked for (booking, posting taster and learning packs, teaching, assessments and reports): performance of a contract with you.
  • Information about your child's learning needs or health that you choose to share: your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Safeguarding: if we are worried about a child's safety we may share information with the local authority or police, as the law and statutory guidance require (legal obligation and vital interests).
  • Keeping financial records: legal obligation (tax and accounting law).
  • Occasional news about Lumina (for example new courses): only with your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Children's data

We take extra care with children's information. We use the minimum we need to teach your child, we never use it for advertising or profiling, and we never publish a child's name, image or work without your separate, written permission. Sessions are not recorded. We do not put children's names, images or work into artificial intelligence tools.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with services that help us run Lumina, under contracts that protect it:

  • Calendly, for booking sessions
  • [Google Meet / Zoom], for live sessions
  • Stripe, for secure card payments
  • [Email provider], for email
  • Royal Mail or another courier, for posting packs (name and address only)

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum.

How long we keep it

  • Taster enquiries that do not go ahead: deleted within 6 months.
  • Learning records and reports: kept while your child is learning with us, then deleted 12 months after the last session.
  • Payment and accounting records: 6 years, as tax law requires.
  • Safeguarding records: kept for as long as statutory guidance requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you or your child, ask us to limit how we use it, object to how we use it, or ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Email hello@luminastudio.uk and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
